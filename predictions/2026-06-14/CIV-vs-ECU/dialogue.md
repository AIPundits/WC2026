This is not a team building toward something — this is a team that has already arrived.

We are not here on a lucky streak.

Côte d'Ivoire have no reliable striker. Wahi, Guessand, and Bonny share four international goals between them. That means their entire attacking architecture depends on wide players finding moments of quality that our defensive structure is specifically designed to deny. Ecuador are compact, organised, and experienced. We will make this match extremely difficult for Faé's side.

**[TURN 2 — CIV PUNDIT]**

**[TURN 2 — ECU PUNDIT]**

The France friendly result is a single data point and should be treated as such. France had rotation and experimented with some combinations. CONMEBOL qualifying ran across 18 months — it is a far more reliable sample size than one pre-tournament friendly.

On our defensive record: Pacho and Hincapié are not Champions League finalists by accident. They have faced pace and width at the highest level of European club football. Amad Diallo and Adingra are good players — but Pacho specifically is built to win physical duels on the back foot. Our system accounts for wide overloads.

Ecuador's approach in this match: control the midfield with Caicedo, push Estupiñán forward when we win the ball, and let Valencia's experience hold the line in attack. A point here — given the group dynamics with Germany — is functionally a win for Ecuador. We do not need three points to advance.
