**[TURN 2 — KSA PUNDIT]**

Bielsa-ball requires extremely precise positional discipline from the centre-back pairing — the entire high-line, compact shape that makes his teams so coherent depends on the two central defenders executing their roles perfectly. Saudi Arabia's 4-2-3-1 is designed to target exactly those central seams. Al-Dawsari, Mandash, and Al-Juwayr rotating around him will create overloads in the space Uruguay's makeshift defence cannot cover.
