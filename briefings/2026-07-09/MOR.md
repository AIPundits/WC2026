## 1. Tournament Journey

Morocco have navigated five matches under coach Mohamed Ouahbi (who took over just three months before the tournament), becoming the first African nation to reach the quarter-finals of two World Cups. Their style under Ouahbi is notably different from the ultra-defensive Regragui era — they are more possession-balanced while retaining a compact defensive block.

**Group C:**
- **vs Brazil (R1, Jun 13):** 1-1 D. A resilient opening performance against one of the favourites. Morocco held Brazil to a draw, showing defensive organisation and counter-attacking threat.
- **vs Scotland (R2, Jun 19):** 1-0 W. A narrow but controlled win. Morocco were efficient rather than spectacular.
- **vs Haiti (R3, Jun 24):** 4-2 W. Morocco's highest-scoring match; attacked freely against a weaker opponent, though conceding twice raised some defensive questions.

**Knockouts:**
- **vs Netherlands (R32, Jun 29):** 1-1 after 120 min, won 3-2 on penalties. A hard-fought contest — Morocco limited the Dutch to just one clear chance and six attempts across 120 minutes (22bet). Diop's 91st-minute equaliser kept Morocco alive. Resilient and tactically disciplined under pressure.
- **vs Canada (R16, Jul 4):** 3-0 W. Morocco's most dominant display of the tournament. A clean sheet and three goals showed real attacking quality when given space. Ismael Saibari sustained a hamstring injury during this match.

**Overall: 3W, 2D, 0L | Goals: 10 scored (2.0/game), 4 conceded (0.8/game) | 1 clean sheet (excl. pens match)**

## 4. Tournament Standing / Form

Morocco have played 5 matches (2.0 goals scored per game, 0.8 conceded). They have shown two faces: a grinding, resilient defensive unit capable of limiting elite opposition (Brazil to a draw, Netherlands to 1-1 over 120 min), and a fluid attacking team capable of scoring freely (4-2 vs Haiti, 3-0 vs Canada). The Netherlands match — won from a 91st-minute equaliser — demonstrated late-game mental strength. Advancing to the semi-final would make Morocco only the second African nation in history to reach that stage (after their 2022 run). Under Ouahbi, the tactical profile is more proactive than under Regragui — but they have never faced France's attacking quality at this tournament. Saibari's absence is a significant blow to attacking depth. (Source: The Athletic, 22bet, FIFA.com)

An inability to score in the group stage is equally telling. But use the knockout frequency table above as your scoreline prior, not the group stage table.
