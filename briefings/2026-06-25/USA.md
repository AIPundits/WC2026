## Round 1 & 2 Performance

The USMNT have been the dominant force in Group D. On 12 June they beat Paraguay **4-1** in their opening match — Pulisic set up two goals before picking up the calf injury that would rule him out of Round 2. In Round 2 on 20 June, they beat Australia **2-0** with two first-half goals, even without Pulisic. The Athletic noted the Australia win gave the USMNT "something it rarely experiences at a World Cup: the ability to approach the final group match without fear." USA were confirmed as Group D winners when Turkey lost to Paraguay later the same day. Pochettino's side now faces a dead rubber vs eliminated Turkey — the key decisions are about rotation, injury management, and yellow card risk.

**Suspension Risk (⚠️ MAJOR):** **Tyler Adams, Antonee Robinson, Folarin Balogun, and Chris Richards** are all on **one yellow card each** — a second booking in R3 means suspension for the Round of 32 (FanDuel / World Soccer Talk / The Athletic). This is a significant lineup consideration for Pochettino.
