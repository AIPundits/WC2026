## Round 1 & 2 Performance

Paraguay's World Cup has been a rollercoaster. They opened on 12 June against co-hosts USA and were beaten 0-4 in a chastening display. In Round 2 on 19-20 June, they bounced back with a **1-0 win over Turkey** — Julio Enciso scoring the winning goal. But the match will be remembered for another reason: **Miguel Almirón received the first-ever red card for covering his mouth** in a confrontation, under the new 2026 World Cup rule. "Ten-man Paraguay clung on" (Guardian) after the dismissal to seal 3 points. The Athletic described it as chaotic but decisive — Paraguay won and USA were simultaneously confirmed as Group D winners. With Almirón suspended, Paraguay must restructure for the most important match of their campaign.

**Suspension Risk:** **Miguel Almirón: SUSPENDED** — red card vs Turkey, automatic 1-match ban (Fox Sports / Al Jazeera / The Athletic). He was Paraguay's squad top-scorer (10 international goals). His absence from the right is considered a potentially decisive disadvantage (Sports Mole).
