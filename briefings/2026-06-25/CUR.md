## Round 1 & 2 Performance

Curaçao entered as historic debutants — the smallest nation by population and area ever to qualify for a men's World Cup. Their opener on 14 June was a brutal lesson: Germany won 7-1 in Houston, with Livano Comenencia grabbing a consolation in the 21st minute after Curaçao had barely laid a glove on the Germans. Dick Advocaat's side regrouped spectacularly in Round 2 on 20 June, earning their **first-ever World Cup point** with a 0-0 draw against Ecuador. Eloy Room made **15 saves** — the most by any goalkeeper in 90 minutes of a World Cup match since records began in 1966. Ecuador had 15 shots on target without scoring (also a World Cup record). Curaçao's disciplined 5-4-1 block frustrated Ecuador throughout. The result was a legitimate historic achievement.

**Suspension Risk:** No confirmed red card suspensions. Specific yellow card accumulation data for Curaçao players not confirmed in published sources ahead of R3. Qualifying for the knockout stage is near-impossible (1pt, GD −6), so suspension risk for R16 is of low consequence.
