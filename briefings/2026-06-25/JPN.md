## Round 1 & 2 Performance

Japan have been one of the tournament's most impressive sides. In Round 1 on 14 June, they drew 2-2 with Netherlands — a strong result against a European heavyweight. In Round 2 on 21 June in Monterrey, they delivered a **historic 4-0 demolition of Tunisia**, becoming the **first AFC nation to score four goals in a single World Cup match** (ESPN). Ayase Ueda scored twice — including a masterful looping header in the 83rd minute — and Ito added a third from Ueda's flick. Tactical analysis (TacticalFootballAnalysis) highlighted Tunisia's defensive frailties: soft man-marking allowed Japan to shoot freely, cross unchallenged, and dominate the penalty area. Japan conserved energy in the second half once the game was won (Guardian). Japan have not lost to a European opponent in 90 minutes since 2019 (ESPN).

**Suspension Risk:** Any player booked in R1 or R2 is at risk of suspension for the R16 if carded again in R3 (The Athletic). Specific yellow card holders for Japan not confirmed in retrieved sources — players will be mindful of accumulation.
