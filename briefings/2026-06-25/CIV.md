## Round 1 & 2 Performance

Ivory Coast returned to the World Cup for the first time since 2014 with a dramatic opener. On 14 June in Philadelphia, they edged Ecuador 1-0 thanks to a 90th-minute strike from Amad Diallo (Manchester United), set up by Wilfried Singo surging from his defensive role up the right flank. The Guardian described it as "the youngest squad at this World Cup" looking to run out of ideas before Singo found space. In Round 2 on 20 June, they faced Germany in Toronto and lost 2-1 in a competitive match — Kai Havertz was thwarted by Yahia Fofana in the early stages, and CIV fought hard. However, the match brought a significant concern: Wilfried Singo was **forced off with an apparent hamstring injury** (Sports Mole) and his availability for Round 3 is uncertain.

**Suspension Risk:** No confirmed suspensions. Yellow card accumulation for individual CIV players not specified in sources retrieved.
