## Round 1 & 2 Performance

The Netherlands were unconvincing in Round 1, drawing 2-2 with Japan on 14 June despite having the squad quality to win. Memphis Depay returned from pre-tournament injury concerns to play, and the Dutch traded goals with a sharp Japanese side. In Round 2 on 20 June in Houston, they were **transformed**: a dominant 5-1 win over Sweden that The Athletic called "the most tactically interesting game of the World Cup" and "a perfect demonstration of how formations and shapes can dictate the flow of matches." Ronald Koeman's high-pressing system exploited Sweden's defensive shape in a statement result. A single point vs Tunisia confirms their place in the last 32.

**Suspension Risk:** Netherlands have significant pre-tournament absences (see Squad Status). Quinten Timber missed R2 vs Sweden due to a training injury — his fitness for R3 is uncertain. For players on 1 yellow from R1 or R2, another booking in R3 = suspended for R16 (The Athletic).
