## Round 1 & 2 Performance

Germany have been the standout team of Group E. In Round 1 on 14 June in Houston, they thrashed World Cup debutants Curaçao **7-1** — matching their legendary 7-1 demolition of Brazil in the 2014 semi-final and surpassing Brazil as the World Cup's all-time leading scorers (239 goals, BBC Sport). Goals from Nmecha (6'), Schlotterbeck (38'), Havertz pen (45+5'), Musiala (47'), Brown (68'), Undav (78'), Havertz (88'); Comenecia scored the Curaçao consolation (21'). In Round 2 on 20 June, they beat Ivory Coast 2-1 in Toronto to complete back-to-back group stage wins and clinch top spot. However, the win was overshadowed by **Nico Schlotterbeck requiring treatment** during the match — later confirmed as a torn medial ankle ligament, ending his tournament prematurely.
