## Round 1 Performance

Algeria lost **0-3 to Argentina** on 16 June in Kansas City — a difficult return to the World Cup stage. Argentina's Lionel Messi scored a hat-trick, but the Algeria goalkeeper — **Zidane** (son of Zinedine Zidane) — made a number of impressive saves, denying Mac Allister and Lautaro Martínez at key moments. The scoreline was comfortable for Argentina but Algeria's challenge was genuine enough for periods.

Algeria were tactically undermined by Argentina's combination of disciplined possession and ruthless counter-attacking. RotoWire noted Algeria "mustered little against Argentina" overall, and Fox Sports described Argentina's approach as *"patient without the ball, ruthless with it, happy to let opponents keep possession in areas where possession does nothing"* — essentially neutralising Algeria's counter-attacking identity. Algeria offered some threat on the break but could not convert.

**Suspension Risk:** No confirmed yellow cards for Algeria players from the Argentina match found in available sources. WhoScored reported *"everyone came through unscathed against Argentina."* *(WhoScored statistical preview)* **No players currently on a yellow card.**
