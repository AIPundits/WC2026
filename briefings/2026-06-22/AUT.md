## Round 1 Performance

Austria defeated Jordan **3-1** on 17 June in San Francisco — their **first World Cup win in 36 years** (since beating the USA in 1990). Romano Schmid opened the scoring in the 20th minute. Jordan equalised through Ali Olwan (50'). Austria regained the lead via an own goal from Yazan Al-Arab (76') after a contested corner sequence — a Arnautović header was initially disallowed by VAR for a Posch handball in the build-up, before Austria earned another corner from which the own goal resulted. Marko Arnautović then converted a penalty in the 90+12' minute to seal it.

Rangnick's side pressed high throughout, overloaded wide areas via Laimer and Mwene, and fed Arnautović with late runs and crosses. The Juvefc preview noted Austria's "fluid, aggressive tactical approach" was effective, though their path to the second goal was somewhat fortuitous.

**Suspension Risk:** **Marcel Sabitzer** (MF) received a yellow card in the 77th minute (unsporting behaviour) against Jordan. *(Fox Sports, The Guardian)*. He is on 1 yellow card and will be suspended if booked in Round 2. **High-profile suspension risk heading into this match.**
