## Round 1 Performance

Iraq lost **1-4 to Norway** on 16 June in their World Cup debut match. Aymen Hussein gave Iraq an encouraging lead in the 39th minute, but Erling Haaland equalised just before half-time (43') and added a second in the 76th minute. Norway scored two more to complete the rout. The scoreline flattered Norway in terms of quality of chances — The Guardian's live blog noted Iraq pushed for chances after conceding, but ultimately the gulf in quality told. Norway had superior tactical organisation and Iraq's defensive structure was undone by Haaland's movement. Graham Arnold's side showed some intent going forward (the Hussein goal suggested threat), but were unable to sustain pressure defensively.

The match was notably clean — Guardian noted: *"Apart from the occasional outburst from Ryerson and a brief spell of irritation late in the first half, this match has been impeccable in terms of sportsmanship."* Fair play points could matter later.

**Suspension Risk:** **Zaid Tahseen** (Iraq) received a yellow card in Round 1. *(AS USA match report)* He is one booking away from suspension.
