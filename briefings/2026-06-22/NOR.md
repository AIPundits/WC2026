## Round 1 Performance

Norway marked their **return to the World Cup after 28 years** with a statement **4-1 win over Iraq** on 16 June in Boston (Foxborough). Erling Haaland scored twice (43', 76') and Iraq got a consolation through Aymen Hussein (39'). Fox Sports' four-takeaway piece characterised it as a "Statement Win." The match was played with excellent sportsmanship throughout — The Guardian's live blog noted the game was "impeccable in terms of sportsmanship" with the first yellow card not appearing until the 87th minute.

Haaland is in extraordinary form: **57 goals in 51 appearances** for Norway, and scoring in **11 consecutive international matches**. Ødegaard and Haaland's partnership was noted as the key driver — The Athletic described them as Norway's "flagbearers, setting standards for others." Coach Stale Solbakken's tactical setup channels service to Haaland effectively and the team's collective pressing was organised.

**Suspension Risk:** The match vs Iraq was almost card-free. No Norway players confirmed as having been booked. **No players currently on a yellow card.**
