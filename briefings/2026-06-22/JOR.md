## Round 1 Performance

Jordan lost **1-3 to Austria** on 17 June — but their debut at the World Cup included a historic moment: **Ali Olwan scored Jordan's first-ever World Cup goal** in the 50th minute to level at 1-1. Jordan had shown "excellent attacking plan" and "good tactical understanding for a match against a top team" (Profile News). Romano Schmid's 20th-minute opener was against the early run of play, and Jordan's equaliser showed genuine quality.

However, the match unravelled in the final phase. Austria's second goal — an Yazan Al-Arab own goal in the 76th minute — was unfortunate for Jordan, and Arnautović's 90+12' penalty rubbed salt into wounds. Austria had a goal initially disallowed by VAR (Posch handball) but Jordan could not hold on once they went behind a second time.

**Suspension Risk:** No confirmed yellow cards for Jordan players from the Austria match found in available sources. However, Yazan Al-Arab featured prominently (scored the OG) — no booking confirmed. **Unconfirmed — verify before prediction.**
