## Round 1 Performance

Argentina opened their title defence with a commanding **3-0 win over Algeria** in Kansas City on 16 June. Lionel Messi was the story, delivering a hat-trick that equalled Miroslav Klose's all-time World Cup record of 16 goals. Argentina had five shots on target in the first half alone; Algeria managed one. Fox Sports described the performance as: *"Patient without the ball, ruthless with it, happy to let an opponent keep possession in areas where possession does nothing."* Algeria's goalkeeper — Zidane, son of Zinedine — made smart saves to deny Mac Allister and Lautaro Martínez but was beaten three times by Messi. Scaloni's 4-3-3 functioned as designed; midfield control was dominant throughout.

**Suspension Risk:** No Argentina players were booked in Round 1. No players currently on a yellow card. (Note: Messi was involved in a challenge early in the match that drew controversy — Goal.com reported it was "100% red card" territory — but the referee took no action.)
