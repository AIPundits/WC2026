## Round 1 Performance

France defeated Senegal **3-1** on 16 June. Kylian Mbappé scored twice (his 57th and 58th international goals), becoming France's all-time record scorer. Crucially, France **struggled in the first half** — Senegal dominated large periods and created genuine opportunities but were wasteful in front of goal. Tactical Football Analysis noted: *"Senegal's tactics frustrated France in the first half, and France's initial attacking structure lacked balance."* Deschamps' halftime adjustments proved decisive — Olise's role shifting to a more central position transformed the match. France then "outscored Senegal in every attacking metric" in the second half. *(The Athletic)*

The win was convincing in the end, but the first-half fragility — particularly against a physical, organised team — is a note for scouting purposes. France were somewhat flattered by the 3-1 scoreline through the first 45 minutes.
