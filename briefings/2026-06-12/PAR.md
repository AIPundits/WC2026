## External Signal

- **Enciso injury is the tournament-defining pre-match story for Paraguay.** BBC Sport, The Athletic, and Sportstar all covered the June 5 injury extensively. If ruled out, Paraguay's most dangerous attacker is unavailable for the opening match and potentially the entire group stage. (BBC Sport / The Athletic, June 2026)
- **Paraguay's defensive structure is their identity** — Goal.com's preview describes the USMNT challenge as "breaking down a stubborn defensive block." Paraguay conceded only six goals in CONMEBOL qualifying, a strong defensive record. (Goal.com, 2026)
- **Set-piece weakness flagged:** RotoWire notes Paraguay "gave up six set-piece situations per game in CONMEBOL qualifying, which is higher than average for a defensive team" — a specific vulnerability USA will look to exploit. (RotoWire, 2026)
- **Alfaro's low-scoring approach:** Paraguay scored only six goals in six qualifying matches (totalfootballanalysis.com). They are built to frustrate, not to attack freely. Without Enciso, the counter-attacking threat is blunted further. (totalfootballanalysis.com, 2026)
