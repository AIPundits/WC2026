## 1. Tournament Journey

**Brazil's World Cup 2026 campaign:**

**vs Morocco (Group C, Round 1) — 1-1 draw:** Brazil's World Cup opened with a frustrating stalemate. Neymar was absent (calf injury). The result exposed some defensive vulnerability and raised early doubts about Ancelotti's system without a fit Neymar.

**vs Haiti (Group C, Round 2) — 3-0 win:** A dominant performance. Vinicius Jr. opened the scoring; Endrick and Rayan added further goals. Haiti became the first team eliminated after two consecutive losses. Confidence restored.

**vs Scotland (Group C, Round 3) — 3-0 win:** Brazil sealed Group C top spot with another commanding display. Attacking depth evident; goals spread across the team. Ancelotti's 4-2-3-1 shape looked settled.

**vs Japan (Round of 32) — 2-1 win:** The most testing match of the tournament. Brazil were overrun in midfield at times in the first half. Ancelotti's bold call: Endrick replaced the injured Lucas Paqueta at half-time, shifting Brazil to a 4-2-4. The switch worked — wider spacing neutralised Japan's press, Vinicius Jr. and Rayan hugged the touchlines, and Martinelli scored a last-gasp winner. Casemiro was also substituted off late, raising twin midfield fitness doubts.
