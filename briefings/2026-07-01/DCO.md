## 1. Tournament Journey

The Léopards navigated Group K — one of the tournament's toughest groups, containing Portugal and Colombia — to advance as one of the best third-placed teams. They opened with a creditable 1-1 draw against Portugal, holding their nerve against a top-tier European side. They then delivered their most complete performance: a 3-1 win over Uzbekistan to clinch advancement. The tactical identity is clear: defend compactly and deep, bypass midfield with direct balls, and release the pace of Wissa and Bakambu on the counter.
