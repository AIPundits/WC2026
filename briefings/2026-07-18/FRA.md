## 1. Tournament Journey

**Group I (1st place):**
- **Jun 16 — FRA 3-1 SEN [WC]:** Controlled opening win. Mbappe and Olise combined dangerously throughout; Senegal had no answer to France's counter-attacking pace. Defensive solidity intact.
- **Jun 22 — FRA 3-0 IRQ [WC]:** Clinical and professional. Three different scorers; double pivot (Camavinga/Tchouameni) dominant in midfield. Iraq offered no threat.
- **Jun 26 — NOR 1-4 FRA [WC]:** Statement performance away. Four goals including a Mbappe brace; Norway's pressing undone by Dembele's pace. France's attacking fluency at its peak in group stage.

**Knockout rounds:**
- **Jun 30 — FRA 3-0 SWE [WC] (R32):** Dominant display; Sweden unable to live with France's width. Olise ran riot on the right. Third consecutive clean sheet over the knockout run.
- **Jul 4 — PAR 0-1 FRA [WC] (R16):** Pragmatic 1-0 via a single goal in a tight contest. Paraguay compressed the space, but France's defensive organisation held. Deschamps' cautious side evident.
- **Jul 9 — FRA 2-0 MAR [WC] (QF):** Strong tactical display; Morocco restricted and shut out. France's balance of defensive resilience and incisive attack showcased at its best.
- **Jul 14 — FRA 0-2 ESP [WC] (SF) — LOSS:** A painful exit. William Saliba forced off with a back injury inside the first half — a defining moment. Defensively disorganised thereafter; Spain's movement exploited the reshuffled backline. France's tournament, otherwise a masterpiece, ended on a deflating note.

**Suspension Risk:** Yellow cards are wiped after the quarter-finals. From the semi-finals onward, **only a red card can trigger a match suspension**. No FRA player faces yellow-card-accumulation suspension for this match.

## 4. Tournament Standing / Form

France finished Group I in **1st place** and reached the semi-final. Full tournament record: **6W 0D 1L** across 7 matches. Goals scored: **16 (2.29/game)**. Goals conceded: **4 (0.57/game)** — an exceptional defensive record with clean sheets in 5 of 6 wins, undone only in the semi-final after Saliba's early injury.

France are the tournament's most prolific outfield unit by goals-per-game. Their sole weakness was exposed against a Spain side that exploited the Saliba injury; in all other matches, they were rarely troubled defensively. Advancing to the final would have been a fitting result; instead, this is a chance to close the tournament with a positive result and third place — France's best WC finish outside 2018 (winners) and 1998 (winners).

### Calibration notes for pundits

- **Tight margins dominate knockout results** — of 25 decisive knockout results, 16 were settled by exactly 1 goal and only 9 by 2 or more. A 1-goal winning margin (e.g. 1-0, 2-1) is the knockout norm; predict a 2+ goal gap only when there is a clear quality and fitness gap evidenced by this tournament.
- **Knockout draw rate (level at 90 min): 16.7% (5/30 matches)** — a draw at 90 min triggers extra time. Do not set `draw_probability` below this rate without strong evidence of a decisive winner.
- **Clean sheets occur in 43.3% of knockout matches (13/30)** — one side failing to score is a genuine and common outcome. If a team has scored in fewer than half their tournament matches, or the opponent has conceded fewer than 1 goal per game, 0 goals for that team is the correct default — not 1.
- **Group stage scoring volume is evidence about individual teams, not knockout scorelines** — a team that scored consistently in the group stage has proven attacking quality that carries into the knockouts. An inability to score in the group stage is equally telling. But use the knockout frequency table above as your scoreline prior, not the group stage table.
- **Group stage draw rate: 27.8%** — relevant only for group stage fixtures.
- **Blowouts are rare in knockout rounds** — they occurred in the group stage (6-0, 7-1) but the knockout data shows far tighter margins. Only predict a large margin if the quality gap is exceptional and evidenced by this tournament, not just reputation.
