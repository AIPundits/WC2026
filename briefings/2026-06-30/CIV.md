## 1. Tournament Journey

Ivory Coast navigated Group E with composure. They opened with a disciplined **1-0 win over Ecuador** — Amad Diallo's late strike the difference — then suffered a narrow **2-1 defeat to Germany** (Kessié scored but Deniz Undav's stoppage-time winner proved decisive). A routine **2-0 win over Curaçao** sealed second place. Les Éléphants have shown defensive solidity under Emerse Faé; their front line has been inconsistent in front of goal, but Yan Diomandé has been the group stage's most electrifying creative player — only the second African player ever to complete 10+ dribbles AND create 10+ chances in a single WC group stage (after Quinton Fortune in 2002), and the first player to do so across their opening three WC appearances since Donadoni and Stojković in 1990 (The Analyst).

**Suspension Risk:** Under 2026 rules, all yellow cards from the group stage are wiped at the start of the knockout stage. No current suspension concerns for Ivory Coast.
