## 1. Tournament Journey

Mexico have delivered the most electric group stage performance for a host nation. **Three wins from three, six goals scored, zero conceded** — their first three consecutive World Cup clean sheets since the 1970 tournament. Javier Aguirre's pragmatic 4-3-3 has been tactically disciplined: 2-0 vs South Africa, 1-0 vs South Korea, 3-0 vs Czech Republic. The standout story: **17-year-old Gilberto Mora** became the youngest player to start a World Cup match in 24 years in the Czech Republic game. Raúl Jiménez was rested for that fixture and is expected to return. El Tri have embraced Aguirre's defensive-first philosophy but shown they can be clinical in attack when the moment requires. The "quinto partido" curse looms — Mexico have been eliminated in the round of 16 at seven consecutive World Cups (and have not won a knockout match since 1986). This, at the Azteca, is their moment.

**Suspension Risk:** Under 2026 rules, yellow cards from the group stage are wiped clean at the start of the knockout stage. No current suspension concerns for Mexico.

### Calibration notes for pundits

- **1-0 is the most common result** — do not require 2+ goals from the stronger team unless there is clear attacking evidence.
- **2-1 is historically over-predicted** — it accounts for a small fraction of actual results; favour 1-0 or 2-0 for one-sided contests.
- **Draws occur in roughly 28.9% of group stage matches** — if your draw probability is well below this, you are likely under-pricing level results.
- **Blowouts happen** — scorelines of 4-0, 5-1, 6-0 have occurred. When one team is structurally outclassed, wider margins are more likely than the 2-1 default.
