## 1. Tournament Journey

**Group G — 2nd place (5 pts, GD +2):** Egypt qualified from Group G in their first World Cup since 2018. Salah — playing through a hamstring strain — was "off-colour" and Egypt "wasted the better chances" in normal time, but the shootout was flawless: all four penalties converted, with Salah chipping a Panenka down the middle as his. ## 4. Tournament Standing / Form

Egypt qualified 2nd in Group G with 5 points (+2 GD) and have now reached the Round of 16 for the first time in the modern era — a historic milestone for the Pharaohs. The tactical base is a deep-lying 4-2-3-1 that protects Salah and Marmoush through a structured midfield block. Egypt are dangerous on the counter-attack and in set-piece situations, but have been inconsistent when required to break down defensive opponents (Iran, Belgium). Advancing here against the world champions would be the greatest result in Egyptian football history. Salah's fitness is the overriding question — a fully fit Salah transforms the calculus.

- **Tight margins dominate knockout results** — of 18 decisive knockout results, 12 were settled by exactly 1 goal and only 6 by 2 or more. A 1-goal winning margin (e.g. 1-0, 2-1) is the knockout norm; predict a 2+ goal gap only when there is a clear quality and fitness gap evidenced by this tournament.
