## 1. Tournament Journey

**Group Stage — Group H:**

- **Round 1 (Jun 15) vs Cape Verde: 0-0** — An unexpected stumble. Spain dominated possession but couldn't find a way through Cape Verde's disciplined defensive block. De la Fuente's side created chances but lacked clinical finishing; only a point from what was expected to be a comfortable opener.
- **Round 2 (Jun 21) vs Saudi Arabia: 4-0** — Spain bounced back emphatically. Lamine Yamal was electric, and the midfield trio of Rodri, Pedri and Fabián Ruiz asserted complete dominance. Saudi Arabia were outclassed from start to finish.
- **Round 3 (Jun 26) vs Uruguay: 1-0** — Spain ground out the win to top Group H with 7 points. **Yeremy Pino sustained a serious shoulder injury** and was ruled out for the remainder of the tournament.

**Knockout Stage:**

- **Round of 32 (Jul 02) vs Austria: 3-0** — Convincing. Spain's press and counter-pressing were relentless; Austria had no answer for their fluidity in the final third.
- **Round of 16 (Jul 06) vs Portugal: 1-0** — The Iberian Derby was tight but Spain were disciplined and decisive. A single goal settled it; defensive organisation was excellent.
- **Quarter-Final (Jul 10) vs Belgium: 2-1** — Spain's only real defensive test of the tournament. Belgium equalised but Spain found the winner. **The only goal conceded in the entire tournament** came here. A test of resilience passed.
- **Semi-Final (Jul 14) vs France: 2-0** — A masterclass. Rodri and Fabián Ruiz controlled midfield completely; Yamal and Williams caused havoc on the flanks. France — expected by many to win — were thoroughly dominated. One of the most complete performances of the tournament.

**Suspension Risk:** None. FIFA clears all accumulated yellow cards before the final. No player carries a suspension risk into Sunday.

### Calibration notes for pundits

- **Tight margins dominate knockout results** — of 25 decisive knockout results, 16 were settled by exactly 1 goal and only 9 by 2 or more. A 1-goal winning margin (e.g. 1-0, 2-1) is the knockout norm; predict a 2+ goal gap only when there is a clear quality and fitness gap evidenced by this tournament.
- **Knockout draw rate (level at 90 min): 16.7% (5/30 matches)** — a draw at 90 min triggers extra time. Do not set `draw_probability` below this rate without strong evidence of a decisive winner.
- **Clean sheets occur in 43.3% of knockout matches (13/30)** — one side failing to score is a genuine and common outcome. If a team has scored in fewer than half their tournament matches, or the opponent has conceded fewer than 1 goal per game, 0 goals for that team is the correct default — not 1.
- **Group stage scoring volume is evidence about individual teams, not knockout scorelines** — a team that scored consistently in the group stage has proven attacking quality that carries into the knockouts. An inability to score in the group stage is equally telling. But use the knockout frequency table above as your scoreline prior, not the group stage table.
- **Group stage draw rate: 27.8%** — relevant only for group stage fixtures.
- **Blowouts are rare in knockout rounds** — they occurred in the group stage (6-0, 7-1) but the knockout data shows far tighter margins. Only predict a large margin if the quality gap is exceptional and evidenced by this tournament, not just reputation.
