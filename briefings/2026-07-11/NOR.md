## 1. Tournament Journey

Norway have been one of the stories of this World Cup — a nation returning to the finals for the first time since 1998, propelled by Erling Haaland at his first major international tournament.

- **Jun 16 vs Iraq (Group R1): W 4-1** — Dominant opening. Haaland and the attack looked sharp from the outset; Norway immediately established themselves as a live threat in Group I.
- **Jun 23 vs Senegal (Group R2): W 3-2** — A scrappy, back-and-forth match. Norway showed character to win despite giving up two goals; Schjelderup beginning to emerge as a key creative force.
- **Jun 26 vs France (Group R3): L 1-4** — A reality check. France exposed defensive vulnerabilities in a comprehensive victory. Norway finished 2nd in Group I and entered the knockout bracket.
- **Jun 30 vs Ivory Coast (Round of 32): W 2-1** — Composed and clinical. Norway managed the game well in their knockout debut, showing maturity under pressure.
- **Jul 5 vs Brazil (Round of 16): W 2-1** — The headline result of the tournament. Norway eliminated Brazil in what was widely considered an upset; the combination of Haaland's hold-up play, Odegaard's creative control, and Schjelderup's direct running proved too much for the five-time champions.

**Patterns:** Norway have scored in every match and have shown resilience in tight matches. However, they have conceded in all five matches (11 total, 2.2/game) — their defensive discipline will be tested by England's attack. The France loss revealed susceptibility to direct, wide-running opponents — a pattern England may look to exploit.

**Suspension Risk:** **Antonio Nusa** is on **one yellow card** heading into the quarter-final. A booking against England would rule him out of a potential semi-final.
