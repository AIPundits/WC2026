## 1. Round 1 & 2 Performance

Cabo Verde have been the fairy-tale story of the tournament. In their World Cup debut (R1 vs Spain), 40-year-old goalkeeper Vozinha produced a heroic clean sheet in a stunning 0-0 draw against the reigning European champions. In R2 vs Uruguay (2-2), they trailed but Kevin Pina scored Cape Verde's **first-ever World Cup goal** (21st minute — making Wikipedia history) before Hélio Varela equalised in the 61st minute to stun the 2-time World Cup winners. The Guardian: Cape Verde's draw with Uruguay "near knockout qualification." Defensively compact, tactically disciplined — coach Bubista has exceeded every expectation.
