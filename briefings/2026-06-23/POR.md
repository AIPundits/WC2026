### Round 1 Performance
Portugal drew 1-1 with DR Congo in their Group K opener in Houston on 17 June. João Neves headed Portugal into the lead in the 6th minute, but Yoane Wissa — who put in an exceptional counter-pressing shift — equalized for the African debutants. Portugal struggled to break down a disciplined DR Congo defensive block, with Cristiano Ronaldo (41 years old, the oldest outfield player in World Cup history) largely pedestrian and described by The Guardian as "pedestrianising behind last line of Congolese players." Roberto Martínez noted afterwards that Argentina had lost their 2022 opener before charging to the title. Portugal must reduce their reliance on routing play through Ronaldo via the flanks; Bruno Fernandes had late chances but failed to convert. It was a concerning performance from a side with clear attacking quality left on the table.
