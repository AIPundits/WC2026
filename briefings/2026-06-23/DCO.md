# DR Congo — Match-Day Briefing | 2026-06-23
## Fixture: Colombia vs DR Congo (Away) | Group K Round 2 | Kick-off 02:00 UTC (24 Jun)

---

### Round 1 Performance
DR Congo drew 1–1 with Portugal in Houston on 17 June — earning their **first-ever World Cup point in history**. **Yoane Wissa** (Newcastle United) became the first DR Congo player ever to score at a World Cup, canceling out João Neves' 6th-minute header. Wissa was exceptional throughout — *The Athletic* described him as "driving them forward, harrying defenders, pressing the goalkeeper, carrying possession with intent and trying to make things happen." Cédric Bakambu (veteran striker) also had multiple opportunities to deepen Portuguese discomfort. DR Congo set up in a compact, disciplined defensive block and exploited the counter-attack ruthlessly on the few occasions they broke. The Guardian called it "a historic result for African football." This performance far exceeded pre-tournament expectations and demonstrated genuine tactical discipline from manager Sébastien Desabre.

**Suspension Risk:** **Chancel Mbemba (CB/captain)** was **booked vs Portugal**. One more yellow card would earn him a match suspension for Round 3. *(The Hard Tackle)* This is a significant factor in how DR Congo manage the Colombia game.
