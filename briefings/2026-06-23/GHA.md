### Round 1 Performance
Ghana beat Panama 1–0 in a tense, chaotic Group L opener in Toronto on 17 June via a Caleb Yirenkyi injury-time goal. Panama were the **stronger team before half-time**, controlling possession and disrupting Ghana's rhythm, but Ghana grew in the second half and managed the game more effectively after the interval. The decisive blow came deep into stoppage time. However, Ghana suffered an early setback: goalkeeper Lawrence Ati-Zigi was **forced off at half-time** with a groin injury, replaced by backup Benjamin Asare. Captain Jordan Ayew (Leicester City) functioned as the tactical fulcrum in attack. Manager Carlos Queiroz set up pragmatically — hard to beat first, dangerous on the counter second, as noted by RotoWire's pre-match analysis.
