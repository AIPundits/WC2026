### Round 1 Performance
Colombia beat Uzbekistan 3–1 in Mexico City on 18 June in a commanding Group K opener. **Luis Diaz was man of the match** — scoring and driving the game with creativity, endurance, and European-level quality throughout. Daniel Munoz and Campaz also scored. Colombia went top of Group K with maximum points. Néstor Lorenzo's side showed strong defensive structure alongside aggressive attacking intent. The Athletic noted Colombia "lean on experienced campaigners" with Diaz pushing 30 but "playing with the endurance of a teenager, European pedigree and the street credentials of a true football celebrity." Johan Mojica at left back was described as "always delivering a professional performance." This was a convincing and reassuring win for a side with genuine ambitions of going deep.
