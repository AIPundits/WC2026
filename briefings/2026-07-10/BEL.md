## 1. Tournament Journey

**Group G, Round 1 — 1-1 vs Egypt (Jun 15)**
A flat start. Belgium were unable to find a winner against Egypt and settled for a share of the points. The Red Devils created chances but poor finishing was an early concern.

**Group G, Round 2 — 0-0 vs Iran (Jun 21)**
The worst result of the group stage — a scoreless draw against Iran that left Belgium's qualification in the balance and prompted real scrutiny of Rudi Garcia's selection and system. Kevin De Bruyne's lack of impact was a talking point.

**Group G, Round 3 — 5-1 vs New Zealand (Jun 26)**
Belgium finally clicked. A cathartic performance with goals from multiple scorers. They qualified as group winners. The margin felt like an outlier given the competition, but it showed Belgium's attacking potential when unlocked.

**Round of 32 — 3-2 vs Senegal (Jul 1)**
The tournament's most dramatic match involving Belgium. They fell 2-0 behind before Rudi Garcia made tactical adjustments. Lukaku and De Ketelaere led the comeback, with Belgium scoring three to win. Garcia's post-match quote: "We know these teams; they lose their tactical control towards the end of the match." *(Goal.com)*

**Round of 16 — 4-1 vs USA (Jul 6)**
Belgium's finest performance of the tournament. Charles De Ketelaere was the standout player of the entire knockout stage: 2 goals, 1 assist, constant movement that dismantled the American defence. Kevin De Bruyne was used from the bench. Amadou Onana suffered an ACL injury in the 21st minute and was carried off — a major blow, though Belgium cruised to a comfortable win.

**Suspension Risk:** Brandon Mechele (CB, Club Brugge) has accumulated one yellow card in the knockout stage. A second booking in this match would rule him out of the semi-final. FIFA resets cards after the quarterfinals, so this is the last accumulation checkpoint. *(Source: ESPN yellow card watch)*

**Notable past meetings:**

- **21st century:** Five meetings, zero Belgium wins, only one Belgium goal in aggregate.

For Belgium to win here, they would need to snap a 40-year drought in competitive fixtures against Spain and overcome a historically dominant opponent. The 1986 ghost provides motivation — and the precedent that it can be done. Draw rate: across all meetings, lower than 25%; Belgium should not rely on a penalty shootout path, though the knockout stage does occasionally go that route.
