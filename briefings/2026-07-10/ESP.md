## 1. Tournament Journey

**Group A, Round 1 — 0-0 vs Cabo Verde (Jun 15)**
Disappointing opener. Spain dominated possession but could not break down a well-organised Cabo Verde defensive block. No goals, no shots of major concern at the other end, but a failure to convert chances set a flat tone.

**Group A, Round 2 — 4-0 vs Saudi Arabia (Jun 21)**
Emphatic recovery. Rodri and Pedri controlled the midfield corridor, Lamine Yamal and Dani Olmo created freely from the flanks, and Mikel Oyarzabal bagged two goals. Spain's high press and quick transitions were on full display.

**Group A, Round 3 — 1-0 vs Uruguay (Jun 26)**
Controlled win to secure top spot in the group. A single goal was enough; Spain kept possession and managed the game. The major blow: Nico Williams substituted off with an adductor injury — he has not appeared in the knockout stage.

**Round of 32 — 3-0 vs Austria (Jul 2)**
Spain's most dominant performance of the tournament. 64% possession, 90% pass completion. Austria were exhausted by the relentless movement and pressure. Oyarzabal scored twice; Spain looked a level above their opponents.

**Round of 16 — 1-0 vs Portugal (Jul 6)**
The Iberian derby. Clinical and composed. Spain controlled large spells, conceded nothing, and took all three points through a single goal. The clean sheet was their fifth in five tournament matches — an extraordinary defensive record.

**Suspension Risk:** Ferran Torres (FW, Barcelona) picked up a yellow card during the 1-0 win over Portugal. He is on one booking in the knockout stage. A second yellow in this match would rule him out of the semi-final. Note: FIFA resets yellow cards after the quarterfinals, so this is the last accumulation checkpoint before the cards clear.
