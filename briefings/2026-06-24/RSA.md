## Round 1 & 2 Performance

South Africa have shown spirit but found the step up to World Cup level demanding. Round 1 vs Mexico (0-2): The tournament opener at Estadio Azteca was a difficult assignment. Mexico were dominant and clinical — Quiñones scored and set up the second in front of a huge home crowd. The match was notable for **three red cards** (ESPN), suggesting it was a feisty, physical affair where Bafana Bafana were physically committed if outclassed. Round 2 vs Czech Republic (1-1): A massive character moment. Czech Republic led 1-0 from the 6th minute (Sadilek) and looked set to win, but South Africa were awarded a penalty in the 83rd minute and Teboho Mokoena converted to salvage a crucial draw. A late equaliser in a high-stakes game shows this team has mental fortitude. Mokoena's heroics, however, came with a price — his booking in that game gave him **two yellow cards across two matches**, meaning he is now BANNED for Round 3.
