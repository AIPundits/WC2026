## Round 1 & 2 Performance

Qatar's tournament has been one of dramatic extremes. Round 1 vs Switzerland (1-1): goalkeeper performed well, Qatar defended doggedly and looked set to lose, then an extraordinary Miro Muheim own goal in the 90+4' handed them a dramatic equaliser — their **first ever World Cup point**. The "Khoukhi late show" (Guardian) papered over major tactical cracks. Round 2 vs Canada (0-6) was a catastrophe. Homam Ahmed was sent off in the 31st minute, Assim Madibo followed later, and Canada ran riot with Jonathan David's hat-trick and a 6-0 final scoreline. Qatar conceded six, the worst single-match concession in their World Cup history. The Ismaël Koné leg break during this match added an ugly footnote to an already grim day.

**Suspension Risk (CRITICAL):**
- **Assim Madibo** — SUSPENDED (red card vs Canada). MISSES Round 3.
- **Homam Ahmed** — SUSPENDED (red card vs Canada). MISSES Round 3.

Qatar are missing their key central midfield disruptor (Madibo) and a defender (Ahmed) — their most physically imposing players against Bosnia.
