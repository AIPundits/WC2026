**Date:** 2026-06-24 | **Fixture:** RSA vs KOR (Group A, Round 3) | **Kickoff:** 01:00 UTC (Jun 25)

## Round 1 & 2 Performance

South Korea have been competitive but inconsistent. Round 1 vs Czech Republic (2-1): Korea started from behind (Sadilek 1-0) but rallied superbly, scoring twice to secure all three points. A gutsy, high-intensity display that showed the Taeguk Warriors' attacking quality — one-touch ball movement that "drove Czechia ragged" (Guardian). Round 2 vs Mexico (0-1): A significant step down. The effervescent pressing and fluid play of R1 was "nullified by Mexico's defensive solidity" (Guardian) — Son Heung-min was substituted after an hour having been kept quiet all game. The only goal came from a Kim Seung-gyu goalkeeper howler, gifting Luis Romo the winner. Korea had the better xG (0.69 to Mexico's 0.48) but couldn't convert. Substitute Cho came closest from point-blank range. **South Korea's fate rests on their final game vs South Africa.**

**Suspension Risk (YELLOW CARD RISK — IMPORTANT):**

- **Paik Seung-ho** — On **1 yellow card**. Another booking = suspended for Round of 32. (SoccerLaduma)
- **Lee Kang-in** — On **1 yellow card**. Another booking = suspended for Round of 32. (SoccerLaduma)

Three key players are one card away from missing the first knockout game. This will affect how aggressively they play vs South Africa, particularly Lee Kang-in who is a central creative influence.
