## Round 1 & 2 Performance

Haiti's tournament debut has been a learning curve against formidable opposition. Round 1 vs Scotland (0-1): Haiti conceded the only goal and were eliminated from contention for the top two early on, but showed fight throughout and were not disgraced. Round 2 vs Brazil (0-3): Alisson was called into action several times — Wilson Isidor drew a good save, and Dominique Simon had a late effort from distance. Haiti did not simply capitulate, but three goals (Cunha brace, Vinicius) inside the first half ended the match as a contest. The Guardian noted it was "a game but limited Haiti" side. They were **the first team eliminated from the 2026 World Cup** (ESPN). Structurally, Haiti adjusted at half-time vs Brazil, showing the coaching staff has tactical awareness, but the quality gap was insurmountable.

**Suspension Risk:** No confirmed bookings or suspensions from available sources. No players on yellow card reported.
