## Round 1 & 2 Performance

Czech Republic have had the classic back-and-forth group stage experience. Round 1 vs South Korea (1-2): Michal Sadilek scored early to give Czech Republic the lead, but South Korea came back and won 2-1. A game that was in Czech hands and slipped. Round 2 vs South Africa (1-1): Again Sadilek scored early (6'), but RSA equalized with a Teboho Mokoena penalty in the 83rd minute. Another lead surrendered late. **Three times now in their last three competitive matches, Czech Republic have thrown away points from winning positions** (Racing Post). They've led in both World Cup matches and left with fewer points than deserved. The goalkeeper reportedly dropped a simple catch vs RSA that nearly cost Czech Republic — nerves or match sharpness is a concern. Sadilek (2 goals in 2 matches) has been the tournament's standout performer for them.

## Tournament Standing

**Group A, 3rd place, 1 point (GD: −1)**  
Results: 1-2 South Korea, 1-1 South Africa

Czech Republic **need** to beat Mexico AND have South Korea lose to South Africa to have any chance of advancing. Even then, GD matters. Mexico have already clinched top spot and are expected to rotate, potentially making this winnable. However, Mexico are unbeaten in 26 home internationals (since 2018) and play at Estadio Azteca. This is an enormous ask. Best realistic scenario: win and hope for a dramatic KOR collapse.
