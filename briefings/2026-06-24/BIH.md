## Round 1 & 2 Performance

Bosnia had a tale of two halves across the group stage. In Round 1 vs Canada (1-1), they showed resilience defending the co-hosts' atmosphere but conceded a late equaliser to substitute Cyle Larin (78') after leading. It was a creditable result on paper. Round 2 vs Switzerland (1-4) was a catastrophe: reduced to 10 men, Bosnia were holding their own until the 74th minute, then conceded four goals in rapid succession. Johan Manzambi scored twice; the collapse exposed brittle squad depth under pressure. The red card — and the psychological fallout — will define how Barbarez approaches this dead rubber.

**Suspension Risk:** Nidal Celik remains sidelined through injury and is out of the tournament. No confirmed additional suspension bans from available sources. Dennis Hadzikadunic returned in Round 2 and should start. No players confirmed on a single yellow card from available reports, but caution advised — specific booking counts unavailable.
