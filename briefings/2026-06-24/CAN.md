# Canada — Match Briefing

## Round 1 & 2 Performance

Canada's tournament has been historic — and heartbreaking in equal measure. Round 1 vs Bosnia (1-1): after falling behind, substitute Cyle Larin equalised in the 78th minute to earn Canada their **first ever World Cup point** at a major tournament. Tactically patient, Canada showed they could grind results. Round 2 vs Qatar (6-0) was Canada's **first ever World Cup win** — and the most goals scored by a CONCACAF nation in a single World Cup match. Jonathan David scored a hat-trick, Larin and Saliba added more. The joy was bittersweet: Ismaël Koné, a key midfielder, suffered a catastrophic leg break (tibia and fibula) in a challenge by Assim Madibo in the first half. Koné underwent successful surgery but **will miss the rest of the tournament**. Jesse Marsch's team must now decide how to reorganise the midfield for what could be a must-win decider for group leadership.

**Suspension Risk:** No confirmed single-yellow cautions identified from available sources. Koné is out (injury, not suspension).
