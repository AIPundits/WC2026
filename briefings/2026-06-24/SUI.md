**Date:** 2026-06-24 | **Fixture:** SUI vs CAN (Group B, Round 3) | **Kickoff:** 19:00 UTC

## Round 1 & 2 Performance

Switzerland have been the group's most eye-catching side despite mixed results. In Round 1 vs Qatar (1-1), they dominated and led through Breel Embolo's penalty (17') but were stunned by a Miro Muheim own goal in the 90+4' — a complacent late display gifted Qatar their first ever World Cup point. The Swiss were sloppy in closing out games. Round 2 vs Bosnia (4-1) told a different story: Johan Manzambi was inspired, scoring twice, and Switzerland plundered four goals after the 74th minute once Bosnia were down to 10 men. The late surge was devastating in execution but it also means Switzerland's form against 11 men, especially disciplined sides, remains a question mark. They're top of Group B on goal difference.

**Suspension Risk:** No players currently confirmed on a yellow card from available sources (SportsMole, SportsMole team news). Clean slate going into R3.

## Tournament Standing

**Group B, tied 1st place, 4 points (GD: +3)**  
Results: 1-1 Qatar, 4-1 Bosnia

Switzerland are **qualified for the knockout rounds**. This fixture determines first or second in Group B. Canada lead on GD (+5 to SUI's +3). A Switzerland win gives them 7pts and likely the group. A draw almost certainly means Canada top the group on GD. A loss would see Canada win the group, with Switzerland still through in second. Switzerland's entire history — **they have never lost a match vs a CONCACAF opponent at the World Cup** (drawing Costa Rica 2-2 in 2018) — adds psychological edge.

## Head-to-Head vs Canada

These teams have met a small number of times historically. The most notable result was a **Canada 3-1 win in a May 2002 friendly** in St. Gallen (Radzinski brace, Stalteri), when Canada had several MLS-era stars. Switzerland have won 2 and drawn 3 in their last 5 meetings per AiScore, though those figures may not include the 2002 friendly. In any event, Switzerland enter as favourites given current squad quality. **No prior World Cup meetings between these sides.**
