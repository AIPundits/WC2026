# Brazil — Match Briefing
**Date:** 2026-06-24 | **Fixture:** SCO vs BRA (Group C, Round 3) | **Kickoff:** 22:00 UTC

---

## Round 1 & 2 Performance

Brazil have been functional but imperfect under Carlo Ancelotti. Round 1 vs Morocco (1-1): Without Neymar (calf) from the start, Brazil were pegged back after conceding Saibari's lobbed opener. Vinicius Jr equalized with a brilliant individual strike (32'). The draw prompted Ancelotti to admit he was "worried" about the start — a candid assessment. Round 2 vs Haiti (3-0): A more comfortable night in Philadelphia. Matheus Cunha (Manchester United) scored twice and Vinicius Jr added a third in a controlled display. However, **Raphinha was forced off with a hamstring injury** after limping off during the match, clouding Brazil's knockout preparation. Neymar didn't travel for this game. The Guardian noted it was "a reassuring victory but far from a free-flowing performance against willing but limited opposition." Brazil have enough to qualify comfortably; the real question is how their injury concerns resolve before the knockouts.

**Suspension Risk:** No players currently on confirmed suspensions. No confirmed single-yellow card accumulations from available sources. Clean sheet on discipline.

## Tournament Standing

**Group C, tied 2nd, 4 points (GD: +3)**  
Results: 1-1 Morocco, 3-0 Haiti

Brazil are **qualified for the knockout rounds**. A draw vs Scotland is almost certainly enough to finish 2nd even if Morocco win their game. A Brazil win could give them group top spot on GD if Morocco draw or lose vs Haiti (unlikely). A Brazil loss is very unlikely to eliminate them given GD +3 vs Scotland's GD 0, but a Scotland win would be a significant upset. Ancelotti will want a controlled performance to preserve players and avoid injuries before the knockouts.
