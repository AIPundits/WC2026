## Round 1 & 2 Performance

Morocco have been one of the tournament's best performing sides, even if the results don't fully reflect the quality on display. Round 1 vs Brazil (1-1): Ismael Saibari opened the scoring against five-time champions Brazil by lobbing Alisson with an exquisite finish, but Vinicius Jr equalized with a trademark strike (32'). Morocco outcompeted Brazil tactically and deserved at least the draw. Round 2 vs Scotland (1-0): Saibari struck again after just **71 seconds** — the earliest Scotland had ever conceded a major tournament goal — and Morocco controlled the rest of the match without breaking sweat. Scotland felt denied a penalty late on, but Morocco were full value for the win. The Atlas Lions look dangerous and organized under Walid Regragui's system.

**Suspension Risk:** No Morocco players currently confirmed on a yellow card from available reports. Clean bill heading into Round 3.
