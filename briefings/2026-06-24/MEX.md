## Round 1 & 2 Performance

Mexico have been the standout host nation at this World Cup — clinical, defensively organised, and relentless. Round 1 vs South Africa (2-0): Julián Quiñones scored and set up the second goal in a dominant opening display at Estadio Azteca — the scene featured **three red cards** (ESPN) in a feisty, passionate encounter. Mexico deserved their win comprehensively. Round 2 vs South Korea (1-0): A less expansive game but tactically mature. Luis Romo capitalised on a Kim Seung-gyu goalkeeper howler to score the only goal. Mexico became **the first team at the 2026 World Cup to secure their knockout stage place**. They've conceded zero goals in two matches — exceptional defensive record.

**Suspension Risk:** No confirmed suspensions from available sources. No confirmed single-yellow card concerns publicly identified. Clean disciplinary slate heading into R3.
