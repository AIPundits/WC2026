## Round 1 & 2 Performance

Scotland's debut World Cup since 1998 has been a rollercoaster. Round 1 vs Haiti (1-0): A composed, professional win to open the campaign — Scotland managed the threat from a limited Haiti side and secured three crucial points, marking their return to the World Cup stage with a clean sheet. Round 2 vs Morocco (0-1): Scotland conceded after just **71 seconds** — Saibari exploiting a defensive lapse immediately after kickoff — and were never in the game tactically. Morocco were "technically on another planet" (Guardian commenter). Scotland pressed in the final 20 minutes and believed they were denied a stonewall penalty, but the late charge ultimately came to nothing. Steve Clarke's tactical approach was questioned ("poor tactics, poor lineup" — BBC reader reaction) but the team did show resolve in the closing stages. Critically, the **historic knockout stage remains in Scotland's own hands** — a result vs Brazil can still send them through.

**Scotland's qualification scenario:**
- A win vs Brazil: Scotland reach 6pts, almost certainly qualify (either 2nd in group or as one of the best 3rd-place teams depending on Morocco result)
- A draw vs Brazil: Scotland reach 4pts — likely the best 3rd-place team in Group C; must hope the broader 3rd-place standings across all groups work in their favour

Brazil need only a draw to be safe regardless. Scotland have everything to play for; Brazil will not want to lose. This creates the potential for an open, attacking contest.

## Head-to-Head vs Brazil

In **10 all-time meetings**: Brazil 8 wins, 2 draws, **Scotland 0 wins**. (Sports Mole)

In World Cup history specifically: **4 meetings, Scotland 0 wins, 1 draw, 3 losses.** (TheSoccerWorldCups.com)

Scotland have never beaten Brazil in any of the last ten encounters. Notable past World Cup meetings:
- 1974 World Cup: Scotland 0-0 Brazil (famous group stage goalless draw)
- 1998 World Cup: Scotland 1-2 Brazil (opening match of France '98, Tom Boyd OG, Craig Burley goal)

**Scotland are yet to beat Brazil in recorded senior international history.** This is one of football's great one-sided rivalries.
