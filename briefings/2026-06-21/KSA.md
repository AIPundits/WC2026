## Round 1 Performance

Saudi Arabia produced a disciplined and tactically astute display in drawing 1-1 with Uruguay. The point was fully deserved and the Saudi performance rightly drew widespread praise. (Sources: The Athletic, The Guardian, Sky Sports)
