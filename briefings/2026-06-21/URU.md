## Round 1 Performance

Uruguay were poor in the first half against Saudi Arabia and conceded from a set-piece sequence: Muslera parried Al Tambakti's header and Al-Amri poked home. However, Marcelo Bielsa's half-time tactical shift — moving Federico Valverde into central midfield — transformed the performance. Uruguay dominated the second half with 27 shots in total and Maxi Araujo finally levelled with a late rebound goal to rescue a 1-1 draw. Goalkeeper Al-Owais was Saudi Arabia's hero with multiple saves. The concern was Uruguay's inability to convert dominance into goals, with all four first-half shots inside the box coming from set pieces — a sign of creative limitations in open play. Darwin Núñez was substituted off at half-time. (Sources: The Guardian, The Athletic, Sky Sports)
