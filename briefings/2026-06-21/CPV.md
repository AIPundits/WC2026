## Round 1 Performance

Cape Verde produced one of the greatest results in World Cup history on their tournament debut, holding European champions and tournament favourites Spain to a 0-0 draw. Goalkeeper Vozinha (40 years old) was the undisputed hero, producing a string of crucial saves to deny Spain's 27-shot, 2.29 xG bombardment. The Blue Sharks sat in a disciplined compact block and defended with remarkable organisation throughout. Their defensive record (fewest shots conceded on target relative to possession given up) on the biggest stage was extraordinary. BBC Sport called it "an incredibly disciplined performance" and the result was widely described as one of the most shocking in World Cup history. (Sources: The Athletic, The Guardian, ESPN, BBC Sport)
