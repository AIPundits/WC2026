## Round 1 Performance

Egypt put in a disciplined and threatening display against Belgium, taking the lead through a stunning Ashour strike in the first half and appearing on course for one of the tournament's biggest early upsets. They held the lead until Belgium coach Sergio García introduced Lukaku in the 66th minute, after which Egypt's Mohamed Hany turned a cross into his own net to level at 1-1. Egypt demonstrated that their defensive organisation is solid and their attacking play, with Mohamed Salah pulling strings, can hurt top sides. The early lead against the Red Devils confirmed Egypt's quality, and their near-miss will provide motivation and confidence for Round 2. (Sources: The Guardian, ESPN, The Athletic, Reuters)
