## Round 1 Performance

Belgium were held to a 1-1 draw by Egypt in a match where they trailed for most of the second half and required a fortunate own goal to level. Egypt broke the deadlock through a stunning Ashour strike in the first half. Belgium's attack was passive while Lukaku sat on the bench; it took Sergio García's decision to introduce the Napoli striker in the 66th minute to change the dynamic. Lukaku's movement immediately drew attention and the resulting cross was turned in by Egypt's Mohamed Hany for an own goal. Kevin De Bruyne failed to impose himself. The performance exposed Belgium's creative limitations when Lukaku isn't on the pitch and raised questions about the starting lineup's efficiency. Winger Jérémy Doku played into the 86th minute. (Sources: The Guardian, ESPN, The Athletic)
