## Round 1 Performance

Iran produced a resilient performance to draw 2-2 with New Zealand, coming from behind twice. New Zealand's Elijah Just scored twice, but Iran equalised each time: Ramin Rezaeian levelled and then assisted Mohammed Mohebbi's second goal. Iran finished the match with 17 total shots (NZL 14), but New Zealand had more on target (8-4), suggesting Iran created greater volume while NZL were more clinical in the chances they had. The contest was described as "end-to-end" with all four goals coming from well-worked moves. The result was disappointing against a side ranked 65 places below Iran in FIFA rankings.

Off-pitch: Iran faced major disruption when authorities ordered the squad to leave the USA immediately after the match, denying normal recovery time. Coach Amir Ghalenoei described Iran as "the most oppressed team at the World Cup." Winger Torabi had separately been denied a visa but later secured a multi-entry visa and joined the squad. (Sources: CBS News, Reuters, The Guardian)

**Suspension Risk:** **Ehsan Hajisafi** (defender) — booked in the 89th minute vs New Zealand. One more yellow card = automatic one-match suspension for Round 3. Source: The Guardian live blog, AS USA.
