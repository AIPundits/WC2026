## Round 1 Performance

New Zealand produced a spirited and entertaining performance in drawing 2-2 with Iran on their return to the World Cup for the first time in 16 years. Elijah Just scored twice — giving the All Whites the lead on both occasions — but Iran fought back each time. New Zealand had more shots on target (8) than Iran (4) across the match and the Guardian described an "enthralling first half filled with end-to-end action." The All Whites pressed high and created genuine danger, with Just's brace from well-worked moves the highlight. The point against Iran (ranked 65 places higher) is a creditable result, but the inability to hold the lead twice is a concern. (Sources: The Guardian, ESPN)

**Suspension Risk:** No New Zealand players confirmed on a yellow card from the Round 1 match. (Iran's Hajisafi was booked; no NZL booking confirmed in available sources.)
