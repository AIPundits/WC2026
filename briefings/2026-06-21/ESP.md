## Round 1 Performance

Spain were held to a 0-0 draw by World Cup debutants Cape Verde in one of the biggest shocks in tournament history. La Roja dominated every metric — 27 shots, 7 on target, 2.29 expected goals — but were repeatedly thwarted by the heroics of 40-year-old goalkeeper Vozinha and a disciplined Cape Verde defensive block. Spain's attack, missing the fully-fit Lamine Yamal for most of the game (he came on in the final 20 minutes alongside Nico Williams), lacked the cutting edge to break down the minnows. Marc Cucurella was Spain's most dangerous outlet on the left. The result evokes uncomfortable memories of Spain's Round of 16 exits in 2018 (Russia) and 2022 (Morocco), both after similar possession-heavy but toothless performances. (Sources: ESPN, The Athletic, The Guardian)
