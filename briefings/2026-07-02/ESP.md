## 4. Tournament Standing / Form

Spain won Group H with a perfect defensive record (5 scored, 1 conceded across the group stage). Two wins and a draw reflects a side that controls matches without always hitting top gear. The winger injuries are the critical concern — De la Fuente's attack is built around direct wide threats, and losing Pino and potentially Williams narrows the options significantly. Rodri's fitness and influence will be pivotal; in knockouts, his ability to protect the defence and set the tempo often determines outcomes. Spain have the squad depth to cope — Merino, Dani Olmo, and Baena are all capable — but the attacking unpredictability is reduced.

Advancing from this R32 match sets Spain on a potential path to meet Portugal or Croatia in the Round of 16.
