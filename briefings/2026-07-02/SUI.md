## 5. Head-to-Head vs Algeria

**All-time record:** Switzerland hold a clear historical advantage — winning 3 of their last 5 meetings and losing none. *(Source: AiScore)*

- **2014 FIFA World Cup Round of 16 (Porto Alegre)**: Switzerland **2-1 Algeria (AET)** — the most significant prior meeting. Switzerland won a tense extra-time contest, then lost to Argentina in the quarter-finals. Haris Seferović scored the winner for Switzerland. This is the only prior WC knockout meeting between these nations.
- **1986 International Friendly**: Switzerland 2-0 Algeria. *(Source: KhelNow)*
- **No competitive meetings other than 2014 WC** in recent history based on available data.

**Key subplot**: Algeria's manager **Vladimir Petkovic coached Switzerland from 2014 to 2021**, including a run at Euro 2020 where Switzerland beat France on penalties. He knows this Swiss squad and system intimately. He now faces his former team — a factor that may give Algeria tactical foreknowledge and could reduce Switzerland's element of surprise.
