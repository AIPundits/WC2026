## 4. Tournament Standing / Form

Algeria qualified from Group J as one of the best third-placed sides with 4 points — surviving on the drama of that 3-3 final-group-game draw. Offensively Algeria are dangerous: Mahrez's direct running and quality is a genuine knockout-round threat, and the 3-3 showed they can score goals from any situation. But 7 goals conceded in 3 games is the central concern — Switzerland's wide forwards (Ndoye, Vargas) and set-piece threat can exploit the same spaces Austria found.

**The Petkovic subplot**: Algeria's manager Vladimir Petkovic coached Switzerland for seven years (2014–2021). He knows the Swiss system, personnel tendencies, and squad depth better than almost anyone. This is his chance for a famous victory over his former employers and a first WC knockout win as Algeria's coach. The tactical foreknowledge could be decisive — or Switzerland's players may be equally motivated by the personal dimension.

Algeria have never advanced past the Round of 32 in the 32-team format era, and they have one prior WC knockout meeting with Switzerland: they **lost 1-2 to Switzerland in AET in the 2014 R16**, when Haris Seferović scored the winner for the Swiss.

Advancing from this R32 would be Algeria's most significant World Cup achievement since their famous 1982 win over West Germany.
