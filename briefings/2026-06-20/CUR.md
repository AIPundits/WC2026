## Round 1 Performance

Curaçao made World Cup history on 14 June. Goalkeeper Comenecia scored against Manuel Neuer in the 21st minute to equalise at 1-1 — the first ever World Cup goal by the smallest nation (by both population and area) ever to compete in the tournament. Germany had opened through Nmecha (6') but Curaçao's leveller caused what the match report described as "a degree of surprise" in the German camp. *(The Guardian)* Germany ultimately ran out 7-1 winners, but Curaçao had their historic moment. Riechedly Bazoer conceded the penalty from which Havertz scored (tripping Nmecha in the box). Germany's xG was 3.91 vs 0.4 — the scoreline reflected genuine dominance in the second half.

**Suspension Risk:** No yellow cards confirmed for Curaçao players from available sources. *(ESPN, FOX Sports, The Guardian checked)*
