## Round 1 Performance

Sweden produced their best World Cup scoring performance in 88 years, thrashing Tunisia 5-1 on 14 June. Yasin Ayari opened with a spectacular long-range strike (7'), Alexander Isak added a composed finish (30'), Tunisia briefly pulled one back through Omar Rekik (43'), before Viktor Gyökeres restored the two-goal lead (59'), Mattias Svanberg extended it (84'), and Ayari sealed the win with a second stunning long-ranger (90+6'). Under Graham Potter's 3-5-2, Sweden's front two of Gyökeres and Isak were devastating, with Ayari's energy from deep midfield a standout. Isak turned provider for Gyökeres's goal, underlining the front two's understanding. Result moves Sweden to top of Group F.
