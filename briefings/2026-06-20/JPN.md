## Round 1 Performance

Japan drew 2-2 with the Netherlands on 14 June at AT&T Stadium, Dallas — a result widely described as deserved. Virgil van Dijk opened the scoring for the Dutch, Japan levelled, Crysencio Summerville restored the Dutch lead, before Daichi Kamada's deflected 88th-minute header rescued a point in dramatic fashion. Tactically, Japan's 3-4-3 was impressive: a tight double pivot limited Netherlands' central penetration, and Japan's transitional play repeatedly threatened on the counter. Goalkeeper Zion Suzuki was outstanding — his composure and distribution under the Dutch press was instrumental in Japan's resilience. The 88th-minute equaliser showed character and will boost squad confidence significantly.

**Suspension Risk:** No Japan players were booked in the Netherlands match. Japan have 0 fair play points (Netherlands have −3), which is why Japan sit 2nd in Group F ahead of the Dutch on tiebreakers.
