## Round 1 Performance

Netherlands drew 2-2 with Japan on 14 June at AT&T Stadium, Dallas. Virgil van Dijk opened the scoring, Japan equalised, Crysencio Summerville restored the lead, before Daichi Kamada's deflected 88th-minute effort denied the Dutch all three points. Tactically, the Netherlands played a possession-focused 4-3-3 but struggled to contain Japan's dynamic 3-4-3 in transition, particularly late on. Koeman's side created early chances — Malen had a gilt-edged opportunity in the 3rd minute — but could not manage the game to the final whistle. Memphis Depay and Quinten Timber came on as second-half substitutes.
