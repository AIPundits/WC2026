## Round 1 Performance

Ivory Coast won 1-0 against Ecuador on 14 June in Philadelphia thanks to Amad Diallo's 90th-minute winner. Ecuador had the better of the early exchanges — John Yeboah and Alan Minda both hit the crossbar in the opening half-hour — but Ivory Coast grew into the match. Their midfield was aggressive and physical: Kessié, Seko Fofana, and Guela Doué all collected yellow cards before half-time, all for reckless challenges. The second half was more controlled. Amad Diallo (Manchester United) was decisive off the bench, ending Ecuador's 19-game unbeaten run with the winning goal. Coach Emerse Faé's side showed resilience but the discipline record in the first 45 minutes was a serious concern.
