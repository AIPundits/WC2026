## Round 1 Performance

Ecuador lost 1-0 to Ivory Coast in Philadelphia on 14 June, ending a 19-game unbeaten run. They were unfortunate — John Yeboah and Alan Minda both struck the crossbar in the opening half-hour, and goalkeeper Hernán Galíndez made a key save from Amad Diallo before the 90th minute. Ecuador's defensive organisation held until the final moment. The defeat was cruel rather than deserved; coach Félix Torres's defensive setup was solid. However, their inability to convert chances and reliance on Valencia for the attacking threat were exposed. Jackson Porozo picked up a yellow card in the 73rd minute.

**Suspension Risk:** **Jackson Porozo (CB/DEF)** — 1 yellow card (73rd minute vs Ivory Coast). One more booking and he misses Round 3.
