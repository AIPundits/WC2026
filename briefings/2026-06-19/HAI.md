## Round 1 Performance

Haiti lost 0-1 to Scotland in their World Cup opener at Gillette Stadium, Foxboro — a narrow defeat in a genuinely competitive match. Coach Sébastien Migné reflected: "You can get punished with one oversight." The Guardian's match report noted Haiti "carried menace without seriously looking like scoring" and finished the stronger side in the closing stages. Scotland's goal came from a set-piece rebound — a slice of good fortune rather than tactical dominance. Haiti's debut in the tournament was respectable given their underdog status.  
*Source: Guardian / BBC Sport / Sky Sports, June 13, 2026.*

**Suspension Risk:** Jean-Ricner Bellegarde (MF) — booked vs Scotland. One yellow card from a one-match suspension. Source: The Athletic box score.

## Head-to-Head

Brazil vs Haiti: Brazil lead the all-time H2H 3-1 in 4 meetings (FWC Times / Sports Mole). Results:
- 1974 friendly: Brazil 4-0 Haiti
- 2004 friendly: Brazil 6-0 Haiti
- 2016 Copa América: competitive Brazil win
Haiti's one win is their lone historical exception to near-total Brazilian dominance. Brazil have never drawn against Haiti.
