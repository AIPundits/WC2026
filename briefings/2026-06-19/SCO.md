## Round 1 Performance

Scotland returned to the World Cup for the first time in 28 years and made it count, beating Haiti 1-0 at Gillette Stadium, Foxboro. John McGinn's 28th-minute goal — a rebound shot that ricocheted off a defender from 13 yards out after Ché Adams' initial attempt — was enough. Scotland "looked impressive in moments moving forward but left gaps for Haiti to attack, which they did" (Guardian). Haiti carried menace without seriously threatening the goal, and Scotland finished the stronger in terms of result if not control. The Tartan Army created a thunderous atmosphere inside a packed Gillette Stadium.  
*Source: Guardian / Sky Sports / BBC Sport, June 13–14, 2026.*

**Suspension Risk:** Kenny McLean (MF) — shown a yellow card in 90'+5' for a reckless/serious tackle in stoppage time. One booking away from a one-match suspension for Round 3. Source: FOX Sports / BBC Sport.
