## Round 1 Performance

Brazil were held to a 1-1 draw by Morocco in their Group C opener at MetLife Stadium — a result that felt like a defeat given their pre-tournament status as favourites. Morocco's Ismael Saibari lobbed Alisson to open the scoring, before Vinicius Junior equalised with "a spectacular angled effort." The Guardian's match report was damning: "for large portions of an absorbing match, Brazil found themselves chasing shadows as Casemiro and Bruno Guimarães struggled to contain a Morocco midfield anchored by the outstanding teenager Ayyoub Bouaddi." Brazil created chances but lacked urgency until Vinicius' moment of individual brilliance. Brazil are unbeaten in their opening World Cup match across 21 tournaments (W17 D4), but this draw does them few favours.  
*Source: Guardian / Sky Sports / The Athletic, June 13, 2026.*
