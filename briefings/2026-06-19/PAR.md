# Match Briefing: Paraguay vs Turkey
**Date:** 19–20 June 2026 | **Kickoff:** 03:00 UTC (23:00 local June 19)  
**Group D, Round 2**

---

## Round 1 Performance

Paraguay's return to the World Cup after a 16-year absence couldn't have begun worse: they were dismantled 1-4 by hosts USA at Los Angeles Stadium, conceding three goals before half-time. Bobadilla scored an own goal (7') to open the scoring, Folarin Balogun doubled it (31') and added a third (45'+5'), and Reyna sealed it (90'+8'). Paraguay's late consolation did little to mask the damage. Football Whispers described it as "thoroughly outplayed in a 4-1 loss, conceding three goals before half-time and exposing weaknesses that had rarely been visible during qualification." Coach Gustavo Alfaro faces a major tactical reset.  
*Source: Guardian / Sporting News / Football Whispers, June 12, 2026.*

**Suspension Risk:** Miguel Almirón (MF) — booked vs USA after a VAR-reviewed simulation caution (the match saw the first-ever "mistaken identity" VAR correction at a World Cup). One yellow card from a one-match suspension heading into Round 3. Source: BBC Sport / Guardian.
