## Round 1 Performance

The USMNT produced one of their finest ever World Cup performances, thrashing Paraguay 4-1 at Los Angeles Stadium in front of 70,492 fans. Folarin Balogun scored twice (31', 45'+5'), with Paraguay's Bobadilla netting an own goal (7') and Giovanni Reyna adding a late fourth (90'+8'). Christian Pulisic and Malik Tillman dominated with pace and trickery — The Guardian noted they "seemed to be in a competition to see who could nutmeg the most opponents." However, Pulisic did not emerge for the second half with a left calf injury.  
*Source: The Athletic / NBC News / Guardian, June 12, 2026.*

**Suspension Risk:** Tyler Adams (MF) — booked in the 59th minute vs Paraguay. One yellow card from a one-match suspension heading into Round 3. Source: Guardian.
