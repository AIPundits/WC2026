# Match Briefing: Turkey vs Paraguay
**Date:** 19–20 June 2026 | **Kickoff:** 03:00 UTC (23:00 local June 19)  
**Group D, Round 2**

---

## Round 1 Performance

Turkey suffered a sobering 0-2 defeat to Australia in Vancouver in their World Cup return after a 24-year absence. Despite controlling possession and creating 30 attempts (only 8 on target), they failed to score against debutant goalkeeper Patrick Beach. Coach Vincenzo Montella chose to start Baris Alper Yilmaz ahead of Kenan Yıldız, with Yıldız introduced at half-time — his injection of quality came too late to change the game. Turkey's conversion rate was alarming: "Australia spoiled Turkey's return with victory; Turkey had 30 attempts without scoring while controlling possession" (football365). A tactical and psychological reset is required.  
*Source: Guardian / Sky Sports / football365, June 14, 2026.*

**Suspension Risk:** Yunus Akgün (MF/FWD) — booked in the 86th minute vs Australia. One yellow card from a one-match ban heading into Round 3 vs USA. Source: Yahoo Sports / Guardian.
