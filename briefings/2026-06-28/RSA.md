## 1. Tournament Journey

South Africa entered the 2026 World Cup in **Group A** alongside Mexico, Czech Republic, and South Korea. They made history by reaching the knockout stage for the first time in their four World Cup appearances.

**Group Stage Results:**

- **Jun 11 vs Mexico (L 0-2)**: A difficult opener against the tournament co-hosts. Mexico's quality told, and critically, veteran playmaker Themba Zwane was sent off for violent conduct — a red card resulting in a three-match suspension upheld on appeal. Bafana Bafana showed spirit but were outclassed.
- **Jun 18 vs Czech Republic (D 1-1)**: Played without Zwane, RSA showed resilience to earn a crucial point. Teboho Mokoena collected his second yellow card of the group stage and was suspended for the next game.
- **Jun 24 vs South Korea (W 1-0)**: A disciplined, defensive display. Maseko struck the decisive goal — "raced on to a through ball" after RSA grew into the contest (per The Guardian) — to deliver South Africa's historic first knockout qualification. A famous night for Bafana Bafana.

**Suspension Risk:**
- Under 2026 World Cup rules, **single yellow cards are fully reset after the group stage**. Mokoena, who served his ban vs Korea, enters the R32 on a clean slate.
- **Themba Zwane remains suspended** — his 3-match red card ban covers this Round of 32 fixture (match 3 of 3). He is unavailable.
- Any RSA player booked today starts the Round of 16 on one yellow (if RSA advance). A second yellow in R16 would trigger a quarter-final ban.

- **1-0 is the most common result** — do not require 2+ goals from the stronger team unless there is clear attacking evidence.
- **2-1 is historically over-predicted** — it accounts for a small fraction of actual results; favour 1-0 or 2-0 for one-sided contests.
