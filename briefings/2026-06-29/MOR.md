**Round of 32 | vs Netherlands | Estadio BBVA, Monterrey | 30 June 2026, 01:00 UTC**

---

## 1. Tournament Journey

Morocco produced one of the group stage's most impressive campaigns, going W2 D1 and finishing second in Group C behind Brazil. On Match Day 1, they drew 1-1 with tournament favourites Brazil — a result that announced their quality, with teenager Ayyoub Bouaddi shining on the biggest stage. They followed that with a 1-0 win over Scotland on Match Day 2, a controlled, professional performance. On Match Day 3, they let their hair down in a 4-2 win over Haiti, with Achraf Hakimi scoring and Ismael Saibari continuing his prolific form (3 goals in 3 WC matches). Morocco head into the knockout stage with 7 points, 6 goals scored, 3 conceded — one of only two African sides in the last 32.

**Suspension Risk:** Yellow card slates are wiped clean after the group stage. No players are suspended or carrying knockout-phase yellows entering this match.

## 4. Knockout Form Summary

3 WC matches: **W2 D1 L0** | Goals scored: 6 (2.0/game) | Goals conceded: 3 (1.0/game)

Morocco have evolved since their 2022 Qatar semi-final run under Regragui. New coach Mohamed Ouahbi (appointed just 3 months before the tournament) has introduced more attacking flair while retaining the defensive solidity that made them famous. The result is a more dynamic but occasionally less organised team in defensive transitions. Ismael Saibari's 3 goals in 3 matches is the headline story. Against Netherlands, the task is to replicate their controlled counter-attacking style — defend deep, exploit Hakimi's runs from right-back, and let Saibari and Brahim Diaz create in pockets. Morocco's Qatar 2022 experience in knockout football is their biggest psychological asset.

## Calibration notes for pundits

- **1-0 is the most common result** — do not require 2+ goals from the stronger team unless there is clear attacking evidence.
- **2-1 is historically over-predicted** — it accounts for a small fraction of actual results; favour 1-0 or 2-0 for one-sided contests.
- **Draws occur in roughly 27.8% of group stage matches** — if your draw probability is well below this, you are likely under-pricing level results.
- **Blowouts happen** — scorelines of 4-0, 5-1, 6-0 have occurred. When one team is structurally outclassed, wider margins are more likely than the 2-1 default.
