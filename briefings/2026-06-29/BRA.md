## 1. Tournament Journey

Brazil won Group C unbeaten, going W2 D1 across three matches. They opened with a tight 1-1 draw against Morocco — a competitive match in which Brazil created opportunities but were held by an organised Atlas Lions side. In Match 2, a comfortable 3-0 demolition of Haiti demonstrated Brazil's clinical edge, though it was marred by Raphinha suffering a hamstring injury. In the decisive group finale against Scotland, Brazil delivered a dominant 3-0 win, with Neymar coming off the bench for an emotional cameo on his long-awaited return from a calf injury. Brazil topped Group C with 7 points, scoring 7 goals and conceding just 1 — the second-best defensive record in the tournament.

## 5. Head-to-Head vs Japan

**In 14 all-time meetings: Brazil 11 wins, 2 draws, 1 loss.**

- **Only World Cup meeting: 2006 Group Stage** — Brazil 4-1 Japan. A comprehensive Brazilian performance, with Ronaldo (2), Juninho, and Gilberto Silva on the scoresheet.
- **Most recent: October 2025 friendly** — Japan 3-2 Brazil. Japan came from 2-0 down to complete a stunning comeback, handing Brazil their *only ever defeat* in this fixture. A psychological reference point Japan will cite.
- All competitive matches: Brazil have never lost to Japan in a competitive fixture.

H2H heavily favours Brazil, but the October 2025 friendly represents a meaningful warning that Japan, now with more European-based players than ever, are no pushover.

## Calibration notes for pundits

- **1-0 is the most common result** — do not require 2+ goals from the stronger team unless there is clear attacking evidence.
- **2-1 is historically over-predicted** — it accounts for a small fraction of actual results; favour 1-0 or 2-0 for one-sided contests.
- **Draws occur in roughly 27.8% of group stage matches** — if your draw probability is well below this, you are likely under-pricing level results.
- **Blowouts happen** — scorelines of 4-0, 5-1, 6-0 have occurred. When one team is structurally outclassed, wider margins are more likely than the 2-1 default.
