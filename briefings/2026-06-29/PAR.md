## 1. Tournament Journey

Paraguay's World Cup group stage was a tale of recovery from disaster. They were blown apart 4-1 by the United States on Match Day 1 — a chastening defeat that exposed their lack of defensive organisation and saw **Miguel Almiron receive a red card**, ruling him out of the next match. Gustavo Alfaro responded by switching to a more conservative, defensive-minded structure. With Almiron suspended, Paraguay held Turkey to a 1-0 win on Match Day 2, the goal coming against the run of play. On Match Day 3, a goalless draw with Australia was enough to see both sides through as two of the best third-placed teams. Paraguay finished Group D 3rd (W1 D1 L1, 4pts), advancing to the knockout rounds by the skin of their teeth.

**Suspension Risk:** Yellow card slates are wiped clean after the group stage. **However, Diego Gómez received a red card** (believed in the Australia match) and is **SUSPENDED for this Round of 32 fixture.** Miguel Almiron, who served his ban during the group stage, is available. *(Source: ESPN / Rotowire)*

## Calibration notes for pundits

- **1-0 is the most common result** — do not require 2+ goals from the stronger team unless there is clear attacking evidence.
- **2-1 is historically over-predicted** — it accounts for a small fraction of actual results; favour 1-0 or 2-0 for one-sided contests.
- **Draws occur in roughly 27.8% of group stage matches** — if your draw probability is well below this, you are likely under-pricing level results.
- **Blowouts happen** — scorelines of 4-0, 5-1, 6-0 have occurred. When one team is structurally outclassed, wider margins are more likely than the 2-1 default.
