## 1. Round 1 & 2 Performance

Austria made a winning start on June 17, beating World Cup debutants Jordan 3–1 in San Francisco — their first World Cup win since 1990. Romano Schmid's 21st-minute thunderbolt opened the scoring, Jordan levelled through Ali Olwan's exquisite strike in off the post, before Marko Arnautovic's injury-time penalty sealed it. Sky Sports noted: "Jordan constantly threatened Rangnick's side — they had the same number of shots (11) and shots on target (4) as Austria — and the ex-Manchester United boss needed his bench to save him." It was not a vintage performance. In Round 2 on June 22, they faced Argentina and Messi's brilliance — a 0–2 defeat in which Messi scored a brace to become the all-time World Cup leading goalscorer. Austria showed resilience but were clearly outclassed by Argentina's quality.

**Suspension Risk — CRITICAL:** Three Austria players are confirmed on 1 yellow card and at risk of suspension for the Round of 32 if booked again today: **Marcel Sabitzer, Konrad Laimer, Stefan Posch** (Source: Sportstar, The Hindu, Jun 24 2026). These three missing a knockout match would be significant. Ralf Rangnick may manage their minutes carefully.

## 5. Head-to-Head vs Algeria

**1 prior meeting in competitive football:** Austria beat Algeria **2–0 at the 1982 FIFA World Cup** in the infamous "Disgrace of Gijón" match — one of the most controversial games in WC history. Algeria had beaten West Germany in the opening round; Austria's 2–0 win (with both sides appearing to play out the result they both needed) eliminated Algeria. Algeria filed a formal complaint. (Source: Tips.GG, Sports Mole, The Soccer World Cups)

Record: Austria 1W, Algeria 0W. 1 competitive meeting. Small sample, but the only precedent is an Austria win at a World Cup.

## Calibration notes for pundits

- **1-0 is the most common result** — do not require 2+ goals from the stronger team unless there is clear attacking evidence.
- **2-1 is historically over-predicted** — it accounts for a small fraction of actual results; favour 1-0 or 2-0 for one-sided contests.
- **Draws occur in roughly 27.3% of group stage matches** — if your draw probability is well below this, you are likely under-pricing level results.
- **Blowouts happen** — scorelines of 4-0, 5-1, 6-0 have occurred. When one team is structurally outclassed, wider margins are more likely than the 2-1 default.
