## 1. Round 1 & 2 Performance

Algeria's tournament opened in disaster: a 0–3 humiliation against Argentina on June 16, with Lionel Messi completing a historic hat-trick. Beyond Anis Chaibi's disallowed goal and an off-target header, there was almost nothing to show offensively. Coach Vladimir Petkovic waited until the 60th minute — at 2–0 down — to make his first substitutions, introducing Mahrez, Aouar, and Amoura simultaneously. It was too little, too late. Algeria recovered with character in Round 2: coming from behind to beat Jordan 2–1 on June 22, overturning a 1–0 deficit with two second-half set-piece goals from Amine Gouiri and a teammate. Petkovic chose continuity (9 of 11 players kept), and the tactical resilience showed. Algeria are alive going into Round 3.

**Suspension Risk:** No Algeria players confirmed as carrying yellow cards into Round 3 from available reports.

**1 prior meeting in competitive football:** Austria beat Algeria **2–0 at the 1982 FIFA World Cup** in one of the tournament's most famous and controversial matches — the "Disgrace of Gijón," where both teams knew a 1–0 West Germany win would eliminate both. However, this was Algeria vs Austria in a group match where Austria won 2–0. (Source: Tips.GG, khelnow.com, The Soccer World Cups)

## Calibration notes for pundits

- **1-0 is the most common result** — do not require 2+ goals from the stronger team unless there is clear attacking evidence.
- **2-1 is historically over-predicted** — it accounts for a small fraction of actual results; favour 1-0 or 2-0 for one-sided contests.
- **Draws occur in roughly 27.3% of group stage matches** — if your draw probability is well below this, you are likely under-pricing level results.
- **Blowouts happen** — scorelines of 4-0, 5-1, 6-0 have occurred. When one team is structurally outclassed, wider margins are more likely than the 2-1 default.
