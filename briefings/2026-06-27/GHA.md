## 1. Round 1 & 2 Performance

Ghana impressed in their World Cup opener, beating Panama 1–0 on June 17 with a focused, disciplined display. The Black Stars then produced one of the tournament's most resilient performances on June 23, holding England to a 0–0 draw in Boston. Ghana sat in a deep defensive block, dealing effectively with England's crosses and width; goalkeeper Benjamin Asare made key saves from Bukayo Saka, and Harry Kane blazed over a gilt-edged rebound chance. Ghana's compact 4-4-2/4-2-3-1 structure frustrated England despite significant possession disadvantage. Antoine Semenyo and Jordan Ayew were the attacking threats. The draw left Ghana in a commanding position to reach the knockouts.

**Suspension Risk:** No Ghana players have been confirmed as carrying a yellow card into Round 3 from available reports. However, Thomas Partey has been flagged with a groin tightness concern that has limited training intensity throughout the tournament — fitness rather than discipline is the key squad concern.

## Calibration notes for pundits

- **1-0 is the most common result** — do not require 2+ goals from the stronger team unless there is clear attacking evidence.
- **2-1 is historically over-predicted** — it accounts for a small fraction of actual results; favour 1-0 or 2-0 for one-sided contests.
- **Draws occur in roughly 27.3% of group stage matches** — if your draw probability is well below this, you are likely under-pricing level results.
- **Blowouts happen** — scorelines of 4-0, 5-1, 6-0 have occurred. When one team is structurally outclassed, wider margins are more likely than the 2-1 default.
