## 1. Round 1 & 2 Performance

DR Congo began their tournament campaign with a stunning result — a 1–1 draw against Portugal on June 17. They held off Portugal's 75% possession, kept their 5-man defensive block intact, and came close to winning through Cédric Bakambu (hit the post) before Yoane Wissa's equaliser earned a historic first World Cup point. ESPN: "Far from holding on for a point, DR Congo looked more likely to win the game than Portugal in that second half." In Round 2 on June 24, they fell 0–1 to Colombia in a match where goalkeeper Lionel Mpasi was outstanding, making multiple crucial late saves from Mbuku and Mbemba to keep the margin tight. DR Congo showed they can contain top opposition, but their attack has not found the clinical edge needed to take points from stronger opposition.

**Suspension Risk:** No DR Congo players confirmed as carrying yellow cards into Round 3. WhoScored and Goal.com note "no injury concerns" for the Leopards.

## Calibration notes for pundits

- **1-0 is the most common result** — do not require 2+ goals from the stronger team unless there is clear attacking evidence.
- **2-1 is historically over-predicted** — it accounts for a small fraction of actual results; favour 1-0 or 2-0 for one-sided contests.

- **Blowouts happen** — scorelines of 4-0, 5-1, 6-0 have occurred. When one team is structurally outclassed, wider margins are more likely than the 2-1 default.
