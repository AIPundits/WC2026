## 1. Round 1 & 2 Performance

Portugal's tournament began with a shock 1–1 draw against DR Congo on June 17. Despite 75% possession, they were unable to break down DR Congo's resolute 5-man defence, and Yoane Wissa's equaliser earned the Leopards a point in a match where Cédric Bakambu hit the post. BBC Sport: "Portugal struggling to create clear-cut opportunities and failing to make the most of their 75% possession." Roberto Martínez pointed to Argentina's 2022 upset as context; the performance was a warning sign. Portugal recovered emphatically on June 23, thrashing Uzbekistan 5–0 — Cristiano Ronaldo scored a brace, becoming the oldest outfield player to start a World Cup match, and the team looked fluid and clinical against a limited opponent.

**Suspension Risk:** No Portugal players confirmed as carrying yellow cards into Round 3 from available reports.
