## 1. Round 1 & 2 Performance

Jordan's World Cup debut has been a proud moment despite two defeats. On June 17 against Austria, they matched their opponents across every statistical category — 11 shots each, 4 shots on target each — and Ali Olwan scored Jordan's **first-ever World Cup goal** with an exquisite right-foot strike in off the post after the interval. Austria's 3–1 win came via a stoppage-time Arnautovic penalty and Romano Schmid's early opener, but Jordan were competitive throughout. In Round 2 on June 22, they fell 1–2 to Algeria, going ahead through Ali Olwan again before two second-half set-piece goals turned the match. Jordan showed character and tactical discipline in both matches — World Cup debutants who have exceeded modest expectations.
