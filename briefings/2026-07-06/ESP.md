## 5. Head-to-Head vs Portugal

**All-time record (41 meetings, from Sports Mole):**
Spain have dominated historically — "losing just seven of their previous 41 battles" across 41 meetings since 1921. However, the recent competitive record is tighter.

**Key competitive meetings:**
- **1934 WC:** Spain 9-0 Portugal — Spain's historic demolition.
- **2018 WC Group Stage:** Portugal 3-3 Spain — Ronaldo hat-trick; Spain surrendered a 3-2 lead in the 88th minute.
- **Euro 2012 SF:** Portugal 0-0 Spain (aet) — Spain won on penalties 4-2. Spain have won both knockout encounters in competitive football.
- **2024-25 Nations League Final:** Spain lost to Portugal — most recent meeting; Portugal won, but this was not a World Cup knockout.

**Spain's knockout H2H advantage:** In the only direct World Cup knockout match (Euro 2012 was a Euros, not WC), there is no WC knockout precedent beyond the group stage 3-3 in 2018.

Draw rate in this rivalry is historically significant — multiple 1-1 and 0-0 outcomes in competitive play.

### Calibration notes for pundits

- **Tight margins dominate knockout results** — of 16 decisive knockout results, 10 were settled by exactly 1 goal and only 6 by 2 or more. A 1-goal winning margin (e.g. 1-0, 2-1) is the knockout norm; predict a 2+ goal gap only when there is a clear quality and fitness gap evidenced by this tournament.
- **Knockout draw rate (level at 90 min): 15.8% (3/19 matches)** — a draw at 90 min triggers extra time. Do not set `draw_probability` below this rate without strong evidence of a decisive winner.
- **Clean sheets occur in 47.4% of knockout matches (9/19)** — one side failing to score is a genuine and common outcome. If a team has scored in fewer than half their tournament matches, or the opponent has conceded fewer than 1 goal per game, 0 goals for that team is the correct default — not 1.
- **Group stage scoring volume is evidence about individual teams, not knockout scorelines** — a team that scored consistently in the group stage has proven attacking quality that carries into the knockouts. An inability to score in the group stage is equally telling. But use the knockout frequency table above as your scoreline prior, not the group stage table.
- **Group stage draw rate: 27.8%** — relevant only for group stage fixtures.
- **Blowouts are rare in knockout rounds** — they occurred in the group stage (6-0, 7-1) but the knockout data shows far tighter margins. Only predict a large margin if the quality gap is exceptional and evidenced by this tournament, not just reputation.
