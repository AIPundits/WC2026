## Round 1 Performance
South Korea produced a commanding comeback victory over Czech Republic, winning 2-1 in Guadalajara on June 11. After conceding Petr Sadilek's opener, Korea equalised through Hwang In-beom and then added a second-half winner to secure the three points. The decisive moment came when goalkeeper Kim Seung-gyu made a crucial low save in the 82nd minute to deny Sadilek's close-range effort with the score at 2-1 — preserving the win under intense Czech pressure. Hwang In-beom became only the third South Korean player ever to record both a goal and an assist in a World Cup match (after Choi Soon-ho in 1986 and Hong Myung-bo in 1994). Coach Hong Myung-bo's 3-4-2-1 proved tactically sound: resilient in defence, dangerous in transition. Son Heung-min led the press effectively. (Source: BBC Sport, Guardian, Sportstar, ESPN, Wikipedia Group A)
