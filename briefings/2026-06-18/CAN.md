## Round 1 Performance
Canada earned a historic first World Cup point with a 1-1 draw against Bosnia and Herzegovina in Toronto on June 12 (BMO Field, attendance 43,002). Jovo Lukic's opener for Bosnia threatened to repeat Canada's 2022 nightmare (scored 2, conceded 12, went winless), but substitute Cyle Larin — on the pitch for just 121 seconds — scored with his very first touch to level the match in dramatic fashion. It was the first time two substitutes combined for a World Cup goal for Canada. Ismaël Koné was the standout performer: completed 50 passes (team-high), contributed 9 line-breaking passes in the final third, and registered 49 high-intensity pressures. Jonathan David was somewhat isolated up front in the first half but grew into the match. Alistair Johnston struggled at right back. (Source: Olympic.ca, Opta Analyst, AS USA)
