## Round 1 Performance
Bosnia and Herzegovina drew 1-1 with co-hosts Canada in Toronto on June 12 (BMO Field, attendance 43,002). Jovo Lukic gave Bosnia the lead, and they looked set to claim a memorable opening-match win until Cyle Larin — entering as a substitute — equalised within just 121 seconds of coming on with his very first touch. Bosnia showed defensive organisation and a clear counter-attacking game plan but could not hold the lead under late Canadian pressure. Sead Kolasinac made a vital goal-line clearance that preserved the draw at a critical moment but limped off late in the match and is a fitness doubt. The Bosnian tactical setup was solid but ultimately undone by a moment of individual quality from a substitute. (Source: AS USA, Olympic.ca, Sports Mole)
