## Round 1 Performance
Qatar produced a stunning last-gasp equaliser against Switzerland to earn their first ever World Cup point as a competing nation. Boualem Khoukhi's header deflected off Silvan Muheim for an own goal in the 90+4th minute, cancelling out the Swiss lead to claim a 1-1 draw at Levi's Stadium, San Francisco. Qatar were defensively organised throughout, using a compact low-block structure and relying on Akram Afif for creative moments. Despite being ranked 50th in the world against 19th-ranked Switzerland, they stayed disciplined and took their chance when it came. The draw was celebrated as a landmark result for Qatari football — and sparked debate about the accuracy of FIFA's offside technology when Qatar had a goal disallowed during the match. (Source: ESPN, Guardian, The Athletic/NY Times)
