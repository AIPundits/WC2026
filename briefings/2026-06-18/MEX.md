## Round 1 Performance
Mexico opened their home World Cup in emphatic style with a 2-0 win over South Africa at Mexico City Stadium on June 11. Julián Quiñones struck early (9th minute) and Raúl Jiménez doubled the lead (67th minute) in a performance that Javier Aguirre's squad controlled — though the match will be remembered as much for its three red cards. South Africa were reduced to nine men (Sithole, then Zwane dismissed), before México's own captain César Montes received a straight red in stoppage time for DOGSO (denial of a goalscoring opportunity) as Khuliso Mudau bore down on goal. Despite the chaos, Mexico were commanding winners. Quiñones limped off near the end, raising a minor fitness concern. (Source: FIFA.com, PBS NewsHour, The Athletic/NY Times, BBC Sport)
