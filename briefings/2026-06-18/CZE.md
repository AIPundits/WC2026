## Round 1 Performance
Czechia's tournament began with a 2-1 defeat to South Korea in Guadalajara on June 11. Petr Sadilek gave them the lead but South Korea equalised through Hwang In-beom and then took the lead before half-time, with Kim Seung-gyu making a decisive late save in the 82nd minute to deny Sadilek's close-range effort when Czechia were pressing for an equaliser. Hlozek was a constant threat — involved in the build-up throughout — but Koubek's 3-4-2-1 was undone by Korean transition speed and composure. The late save was the difference between a draw and the loss. (Source: BBC Sport live blog, Guardian, Sportstar)
