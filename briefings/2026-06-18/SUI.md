## Round 1 Performance
Switzerland led their opening match against Qatar at Levi's Stadium (San Francisco, attendance 67,966) and appeared to be heading for a comfortable win before Silvan Muheim conceded a deflected own goal from Boualem Khoukhi's stoppage-time header (90+4'), giving Qatar a stunning 1-1 draw. It was a devastating late blow for Yakin's side. Granit Xhaka and Ricardo Rodriguez both made record-equalling 13th World Cup appearances for Switzerland, passing Xherdan Shaqiri's mark. Without Shaqiri (retired from international football in July 2024), Switzerland lacked the individual creative spark to put the match to bed when ahead. Breel Embolo and Ruben Vargas showed intent but failed to add a second goal. (Source: Guardian match report, ESPN, World Soccer Talk)
