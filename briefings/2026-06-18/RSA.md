## Round 1 Performance
Bafana Bafana suffered a 2-0 defeat to co-hosts Mexico in the tournament opener on June 11 at Mexico City Stadium — a match that descended into extraordinary chaos with three red cards. Julián Quiñones opened the scoring in the 9th minute; Raúl Jiménez doubled it in the 67th. South Africa's Sphephelo Sithole was dismissed first; Themba Zwane then received a second red card, leaving Bafana with nine men for the final stretch. Despite the numerical disadvantage, Khuliso Mudau remained lively on the right flank and drew Mexico's third red card in stoppage time (Montes, DOGSO). Manager Hugo Broos said after the match: "I have a group of guys who learn very quickly and we will see a totally different team against Czechia." (Source: 101greatgoals.com, BBC Sport, PBS NewsHour)
