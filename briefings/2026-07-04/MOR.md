## 1. Tournament Journey

**Group C — Finished 2nd (behind Brazil)**

- **Round 1 vs Brazil (Jun 13) — 1-1 draw**: Morocco made a fast start — Neil El Aynaoui had an effort blocked early. They held Brazil to a draw in one of the tournament's most anticipated group openers. A resilient performance establishing defensive solidity.
- **Round 2 vs Scotland (Jun 20) — 1-0 win**: Clean sheet victory. Morocco were compact and clinical, winning narrowly but comfortably. Demonstrated the defensive discipline associated with the squad's Atlas Lions identity.
- **Round 3 vs Haiti (Jun 25) — 4-2 win**: High-scoring affair with Morocco's attacking quality on full display, though the two goals conceded flagged defensive vulnerability when Haiti pressed. Brahim Diaz and attacking midfielders were effective.

**Knockout Stage:**

- **Round of 32 vs Netherlands (Jun 29) — 1-1, advanced 3-2 on penalties**: Netherlands were narrow group winners and heavily fancied. Morocco matched them across 90 minutes and extra time, then held their nerve in the shootout. GK Bono (Bounou) was composed throughout. The penalty win reinforces Morocco's mental fortitude in knockout football — they have now won two successive shootouts in major tournaments (2022 WC R16 vs Spain was also won on penalties).

**Suspension Risk:** **Issa Diop (CB)** — picked up his yellow card just two minutes into the second half against Netherlands in the R32. A second booking in this fixture would rule him out of the quarterfinal. He is the only Morocco player on a yellow card heading into the R16. *(Source: Squawka)*

## 4. Tournament Standing / Form

Morocco advanced from Group C in second place, behind Brazil. Across 4 WC matches: **7 goals scored** (1.75/game), **4 conceded** (1.0/game). Record: W2 D2 L0. They have not trailed in this tournament — all goals conceded came when already ahead or level. Penalty shootout expertise is a meaningful differentiator: Morocco eliminated Spain and Portugal in 2022 on penalties, and now the Netherlands in 2026. Advancing here would set up a potential quarterfinal against France or Paraguay.

Coach Mohamed Ouahbi — appointed March 2026 after Walid Regragui was sacked — won the U-20 World Cup with Morocco in 2025. He has introduced a more front-foot pressing approach while retaining the defensive organisation of the 2022 era.
